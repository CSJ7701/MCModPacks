** Downed
Makes death more punishing.
New items:
- Revival Token: Use when near a downed player to revive them instantly, with no health penalty
- Health Token: Use to increase your maximum health

New functionality:
- Once you die, you won't be able to respawn until a configurable timer expires. If you try to respawn before then, you'll be placed into spectator near your death site. During this period, you can be revived by a player using a revival token, or an admin command. If revived this way, you will respawn at the site of your death with some brief buffs.
- If you die and the timer expires before you are revived, you can respawn as normal. You will lose 1 max heart and respawn with some brief debuffs.
- You cannot lose all your hearts. You will only lose max health to a configurable minimum

** Lectern Particles
You can use lecterns and writable books (unsigned. If you sign them this sometimes causes issues) to define particles at a point, within an area, or around a player.
Particle definitions work worldwide, whether or not the lectern is loaded (in general the lectern must be loaded to *start* the effect, but then can be unloaded once the particles are going. I'm working on making this start immediately when a chunk is loaded, but for now you have to right click the lectern to start it).

This works based on some specific syntax you put into the book.
- "particle" - required. Defines the particle you want to spawn. 
- "mode" - defaults to "point". Can be "point", "area", or "player".
- "area" - defaults to "[1, 1, 1]". Specifies the area particles should spawn in for "area" and "player" mode.
- "offset" - defaults to "[0, 1, 0]". Specifies the offset from the lectern (in "area" and "point" mode) or player (in "player" mode) for the particles to spawn
- "count" - specifies the number of particles to spawn per tick. Defaults to "2" (will spawn every other tick).
- "freq" - specifies the frequency with which particles should spawn. Lower numbers make particles spawn faster, with a minimum of 1. Defaults to "10".
- "speed" - specifies the velocity imparted to particles when they spawn. High values can cause graphical weirdness. Defaults to "0.01".
- "player" - the username of the player particles should follow in "player" mode. Defaults to "".

** Survival Light Blocks
Makes minecrafts "light" blocks craftable using 1 torch and 1 glass block.
Light blocks are invisible in survival unless holding a light block in your main hand, but will still generate light.
When holding a light block in your main hand, you will see end rod particles where you placed the block.

You cannot break the block if you are not holding a light block in your main hand.
Currently, breaking a placed light block does not drop the block itself.

** Resizing Mushrooms
Adds 2 new foods, a "growth morsel" and a "shrink morsel".
Eating a growth morsel will increase your player size, along with some other properties (step height, speed, jump height, damage, etc).
Eating a shrink morsel will decrease the same properties.
